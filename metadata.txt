    ENGLISH / EN
    Poprawka odwzorowawcza PL-2000 helps check the areas of existing and proposed parcels for surveying and cadastral work in Poland.
    It implements § 16(2) and the formula in Annex 3 of the Regulation of 27 July 2021 on the land and building register (Journal of Laws 2024, item 219, as amended). Results are shown in m² and ha, with a reporting precision of 0.0001 ha.
    This calculation differs from QGIS's native Cartesian area and geodesic measurement. All three values are shown for comparison.
    One parcel: Plugins → Poprawka odwzorowawcza PL-2000. Multiple parcels: the same name in Processing. The single-parcel report can be saved as Markdown.
    Geometry is checked; optionally, its copy can be repaired. Results for invalid geometry without repair are diagnostic only.
    Runs locally, does not send data outside QGIS and does not modify the source layer. Uses only libraries supplied with QGIS and the Python standard library, with no additional dependencies.
    Developed through vibe coding, with AI assistance. The author accepts no responsibility for results or consequences of their use, to the extent permitted by law. Verify the data, CRS and current regulations before use.
    Local checks following QGIS guidelines — no issues found:
    - Bandit: Python code security.
    - detect-secrets: passwords, keys and tokens.
    - Flake8: code errors and quality.
    - ZIP analysis: contents, structure and file permissions.
    Additional checks: Ruff, pip-audit, calculations, geometry, export and reproducible packaging. These are local checks, not QGIS certification.
    Guidelines: https://plugins.qgis.org/docs/security-scanning/tools
    Regulations: https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2024/219/ogl
    QGIS 3.40–3.x. Polish interface. Coverage: Poland, PL-2000 zones 5–8.
